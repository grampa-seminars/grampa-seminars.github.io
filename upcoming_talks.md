## The classical limit of the Magnus expansion
Speaker: Pablo Vives Matasan
Date: 26 October 2026, 14:00 CET

In this talk, we will look at tree-level Magnus Compton amplitudes at arbitrary multiplicity and how their classical limit is governed by the algebra of permutations. After reviewing the Magnus expansion and its diagrammatic rules, we will focus on a scalar toy model consisting of a massive scalar interacting with a massless one. By working in Schwinger parameter space, we reframe the classical expansion as a series of identities involving the first Eulerian projector in the algebra of permutations. Within this framework, we show how all hyperclassical terms cancel before integration, and how all classical terms precisely match worldline quantum field theory. Along the way, we uncover a new formula for Murua coefficients and a new identity they satisfy, valid for any tree or acyclic loop graph. Time permitting, I will comment on connections to existing literature on the exponentiation of multiple Wilson lines.
