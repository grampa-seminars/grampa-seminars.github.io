## Gravitational Radiation form Shockwave Scattering
Speaker: Emanuele Rosi
Date: 23 November 2026, 14:00 CET

I will talk about the collision of two massless particles in general relativity at high energies, where the standard perturbative approaches fail. Therefore, I treat one particle as a perturbation on the shockwave background generated by the other using the Self-Force Effective Field Theory. After including radiation effects through the Schwinger-Keldysh path integral, I construct the effective action up to second order in the expansion parameter and, with a targeted choice of gauge, I prove the all-order vanishing of the recoil operators. Then I compute the exact graviton propagator on the background and use it to produce the waveform at first perturbative order to and all loops. Finally, I match its soft expansion with the classical soft graviton theorems of Sahoo and Sen, and show how the Gruzinov-Veneziano energy spectrum is obtained from this waveform.
