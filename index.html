Announcements, speaker information, and connection details go out on a Google Group. To join:

  1. Press the button. It opens an email to the subscribe address.
  2. Send it. Body can be empty.
  3. Reply to the confirmation message Google Groups sends back. Important: because of a bug on Google's side, the "Join group" button in that email does not work. You have to reply to the email; even a blank reply will subscribe you.
